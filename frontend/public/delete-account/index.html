Delete your Swaadesi account

Swaadesi · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Swaadesi account and the personal data associated with it. Swaadesi is a hyperlocal home-cooked food marketplace available at https://swaadesi.org and on Google Play.

How to request deletion

Email us to delete your account:

  1. Send an email to hello@swaadesi.org with the subject "Delete my Swaadesi account".
  2. In the email, include the mobile phone number registered with your Swaadesi account, so we can verify the request.
  3. We will verify ownership of that number and delete your account. Requests are processed within 30 days, and we will confirm by email when it is done.

For your security, we may contact you on your registered number to confirm the request before deleting the account.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your name and mobile phone number
  • Your saved location and address
  • Your notification (push) subscriptions
  • For cooks: your dish listings and uploaded dish photos
  • Your order history associated with the account

What data may be retained

We may retain a limited set of records where required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes — for example, transaction and payment records (processed by our payment partner, Razorpay). Such records are kept only for as long as the law requires and are then deleted. Retained records are not used to re-identify you for marketing or Service personalisation.

Questions
