Privacy Policy

Swaadesi · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swaadesi ("Swaadesi", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Swaadesi website and Android app (together, the "Service") available at https://swaadesi.org. Swaadesi is a hyperlocal marketplace that connects people with home cooks in their neighbourhood for fresh home-cooked food and tiffin. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service:

DataWhy we collect it
Mobile phone number To create your account and sign you in securely using a one-time password (OTP).
Name To identify you to cooks and customers for orders and pickup.
Location (approximate latitude/longitude and the address you set) To show you home-cooked food available near you, calculate distance to a cook, set a cook's kitchen/pickup point, and notify you when food is listed nearby. You choose your location in the app; precise device GPS is used only when you tap "Use my current location".
Order information Dishes, quantities, pickup codes and order status, to process and fulfil your orders.
Payment information Payments are processed by our payment partner Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details on our servers; we receive only a payment confirmation and reference. Cooks provide a UPI ID to receive payouts.
Dish photos (cooks only) Uploaded by cooks to show their dishes in the feed.
Notification subscription If you enable notifications, we store the push subscription provided by your browser so we can alert you about new dishes nearby. You can turn this off anytime in your profile.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show home-cooked food near your chosen location and enable ordering and pickup.
  • To process payments and, for cooks, to settle payouts.
  • To send order-related messages and, if enabled, notifications about new dishes nearby.
  • To operate, maintain, protect, and improve the Service and prevent abuse.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

  • Between users to fulfil an order: when you place an order, the relevant cook sees your name and order details; for pickup you receive the cook's pickup location and a pickup code. Your phone number is not shared between users through the app.
  • Payment processing: with Razorpay, our payment gateway, to process payments and payouts, subject to Razorpay's privacy policy.
  • Notifications: notification messages are delivered through your device's browser push service (for example, Google, Apple, or Mozilla).
  • Service providers / infrastructure: with hosting and database providers (such as Amazon Web Services and Supabase) that store data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the Service.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal data (see "Contact us").

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • Update your name and location at any time in your profile.
  • Turn notifications on or off at any time in your profile.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Children

The Service is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the Service. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Policy or your data, or to request deletion of your account, contact us at hello@swaadesi.org.
